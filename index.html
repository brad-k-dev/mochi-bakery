FAQ

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • How do I unlock flavors and customers? Reach goals for lines popped, customers served, best score and rounds played. The Mochi Book shows each goal and your progress.
  • No sound? Check the speaker button at the top right of the game, your device volume and Silent mode.
  • No vibration? Check Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics.
  • Any purchases or ads? None, and no internet connection is needed.

常见问题

  • 游戏进度保存在哪里？ 只保存在您的设备上。删除应用后进度也会删除。
  • 怎样解锁新口味和新客人？ 达成消除行数、招待客人数、最高分和游戏局数的目标即可解锁。可以在麻薯图鉴中查看每个目标和进度。
  • 没有声音？ 请检查游戏右上角的扬声器按钮、设备音量和静音开关。
  • 没有震动？ 请检查 设置 → 声音与触感 → 系统触感反馈 是否开启。
  • 有付费或广告吗？ 没有，也不需要联网。
