Privacy Policy

Mochi Bakery does not collect any personal data.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no account system, no in-app purchases and no third-party SDKs in this app. The app never connects to the internet, and we don't run any servers.

What is stored on your device

  • Your best score, the game in progress, Mochi Book progress and sound setting are saved only in the app's own storage on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes

Any change will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隐私政策

麻薯烘焙坊不收集任何个人信息。
